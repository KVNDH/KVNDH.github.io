BrushWorks

Filled with care, not talent

Pick a photo you like and rub the screen. You do not have to draw well; the canvas fills with the time you put in.

Download on the App Store

01

The color comes from the photo

There is no palette. Rub the screen and BrushWorks reads the photo at that exact spot, then lays that color down as paint. All you choose is a brush and a size.

  • Color lifted from the spot you rub
  • Paint with visible thickness
  • Eye button to check the original photo
  • GlazeSheer color that mixes as it layers
  • BristleStreaks that follow your stroke
  • KnifeThick paint with a wet sheen

Brush size9 steps

Underlay strength5 steps

02

Three brushes

Pick a brush and change its size as you work. Paint over a spot and the last brush through leaves its grain. Turn down Underlay strength and the photo fades; at the lowest step, Blind mode, it is hidden.

  • Glaze, Bristle and Knife
  • Brush size in 9 steps
  • Underlay strength in 5 steps

03

Every stroke on record

Every dab is recorded. Replay plays the painting back from the start, and Time-lapse turns it into a short video.

Replay
  • Replay at 1×, 2× or 4×
  • Vertical 9:16 time-lapse video
  • A record of dabs and time painted

04

Paint without signing up

Open the app, pick a photo and start painting. There is no account to create and nothing to sign in to.

  • No account
  • No sign-in
  • Works offline
