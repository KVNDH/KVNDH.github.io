TailorColor

A look built color by color

Dress the mannequin color by color and find a look that holds together.

01

Six slots, one color each

Top, bottom, shoes, hat, outer and bag make six slots. Tap one and a sheet opens with garments above and colors below, and what you pick lands on the mannequin right away. Colors come from a set of 24. Neutrals, denim and earth tones carry the base, and accents sit on top of them.

  • Six slots: top, bottom, shoes, hat, outer, bag
  • 24 colors across neutrals, denim, earth and accents
  • Hat, outer and bag switch on and off
  • Another combinationMove down the ranked list
  • LockHold the color in that slot
  • Let TailorColor chooseHand the slot back to the ranking

RulesEleven

Looks preparedUp to 12

02

Flip through them in order

Eleven rules score every combination, and the strongest twelve are laid out in advance. Each tap on Another combination moves one place down that list, and the end wraps around to the start. Only the best look for each pairing of bottom and shoes survives, so what comes up next reads as plainly different. A line above the mannequin says why this one works.

  • Eleven rules weighing contrast and color count
  • Up to 12 looks, ranked
  • Lock holds one color while the rest change

03

Take a color from a photo

Photograph a piece you already own, or anything whose color caught your eye, then tap the spot you want. The app reads the color there and swaps in the nearest of the 24. The photo sits on screen while you choose and is not kept in the app.

  • Choose from your photos
  • Take one on the spot

04

Keep the ones you like

Save this look drops all six slots into Saved. When a single piece is the part worth keeping, put it in the Closet instead. Neither has a limit, so they pile up and stay there. Share this look renders the current outfit at 1080 x 1350 and hands it to the share sheet.

  • Saved holds all six slots together
  • Closet holds one garment at a time
  • Shared as a 1080 x 1350 image
