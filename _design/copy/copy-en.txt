## 자주 묻는 질문
[brushworks.faq.0.q] Where your photo is kept
[brushworks.faq.0.a.0] BrushWorks keeps a copy of the photo you picked in the app's storage, so it can bring your painting back the next time you open the app. The copy is deleted when you pick another photo or tap Start over in Settings.
[brushworks.faq.0.a.1] The iOS photo picker hands the app only the one photo you choose. Saving a finished painting uses add-only access to Photos.
[brushworks.faq.1.q] Why you paint the whole canvas yourself
[brushworks.faq.1.a.0] BrushWorks is for painting by hand, so there is no one-tap conversion. That is also why the time-lapse ends with how many strokes you made and how long you painted.
[brushworks.faq.2.q] Why switching photos clears the painting
[brushworks.faq.2.a.0] Each stroke takes its color from the photo whenever the painting is redrawn. Old strokes on a new photo would show colors they never had, so the canvas is cleared. If you have painted anything, the app asks first.
[brushworks.faq.2.a.1] Undo cannot bring it back. Save your painting before you switch photos.
[brushworks.faq.3.q] Time-lapse length
[brushworks.faq.3.a.0] The free time-lapse is 10 seconds long, in a vertical 9:16 frame. With Pro you choose a length from 10 to 90 seconds.
[brushworks.faq.3.a.1] To watch the whole process, use Replay in the app. Pick a speed from 1× to 4×, and drag sideways across the canvas to jump to any point.
[brushworks.faq.4.q] Restoring Pro
[brushworks.faq.4.a.0] On a new device or after reinstalling the app, open Settings and tap Restore Purchases in the Pro section. With the same Apple Account, you are not charged again.
[brushworks.faq.5.q] Internet connection
[brushworks.faq.5.a.0] Painting, saving and time-lapses work without an internet connection. Buying Pro and restoring purchases need a connection to the App Store.

[snapline.faq.0.a.0] Each line has three points. The two ends sit on the edge of the photo, and the middle one moves freely. Drag the middle point and the line bends while its ends stay put. Drag an edge point and it slides along that edge, tilting the line.

## 자주 묻는 질문
[dice-master.faq.0.q] Where settings and history are kept
[dice-master.faq.0.a.0] Dice settings, My Presets and Roll History are kept in the app's own storage. Deleting the app deletes them as well. iCloud sync is not supported.
[dice-master.faq.0.a.1] Reset App at the bottom of App Settings erases all settings, presets and roll history, and it cannot be undone. A Pro purchase is not affected.
[dice-master.faq.1.q] A die that is not in the list
[dice-master.faq.1.a.0] Tap the faces chip at the top of the screen to open Dice Settings. Tap the pencil box and enter any number from 2 to 100 under Custom Faces to roll a die with that many sides. Dice beyond 10 faces need Pro.
[dice-master.faq.1.a.1] The 3D view supports 2 to 20 sides. Larger dice are shown in 2D.
[dice-master.faq.2.q] App language
[dice-master.faq.2.a.0] The app starts in your device language. To change it, open Language in App Settings and pick one of 13 languages. The switch happens inside the app right away.
[dice-master.faq.3.q] What needs Pro
[dice-master.faq.3.a.0] Dice beyond 10 faces, more than 10 dice at once, the Neon, Thunder, Gold and Flame skins, and custom icons need Pro. So do verdicts after you change a Quick Preset's rules. The d20 Check preset rolls without Pro.
[dice-master.faq.3.a.1] Without Pro you can save 2 presets, and Roll History and Statistics show your latest 50 rolls. Older rolls are still kept in the app, and all of them appear with Pro.
[dice-master.faq.4.q] Restoring Pro
[dice-master.faq.4.a.0] If you switched devices or reinstalled the app, tap the gear at the top right and open App Settings. Tap DiceMaster Pro at the top, then Restore Purchase near the bottom of the sheet. With the same Apple Account you are not charged again.
[dice-master.faq.5.q] Internet connection
[dice-master.faq.5.a.0] Rolling, history and presets work without an internet connection. Only buying and restoring Pro need a connection to the App Store.
[dice-master.faq.6.q] Turning off Shake to Roll
[dice-master.faq.6.a.0] Turn off Shake to Roll under Controls in App Settings, and shaking the device will no longer roll the dice.
